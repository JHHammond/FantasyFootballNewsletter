    How this page is made

    Every Tuesday we collect the week that just finished for every league connected to The Commissioner’s Desk, on Sleeper, ESPN and Yahoo. Every starting lineup is re-scored in plain PPR from the same stat lines, so a 130 in a standard league on ESPN means the same as a 130 in a PPR league on Sleeper. Team and manager names never appear here; a team is only ever “a 12-team PPR league on Sleeper.”

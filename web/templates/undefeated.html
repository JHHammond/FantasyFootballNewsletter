How the Watch is kept

Every redraft league connected to The Commissioner’s Desk, on Sleeper, ESPN and Yahoo: {{ '{:,}'.format(S.leagues) }} of them through week {{ u.through }}. Dynasty leagues are left out. A team is unbeaten until it loses or ties. A league counts only once every one of its weeks is in, so nobody’s perfect by a gap in our data. Scores are compared in plain PPR, all-play uses each league’s own scoring, and the winless and the country leave out {{ '{:,}'.format(S.abandoned) }} abandoned rosters (fewer than half the starters took the field in some week). Teams are described, never named.

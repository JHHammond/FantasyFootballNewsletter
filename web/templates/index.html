How it works

  1. Make an account. Email and a password, so your papers are all in one place.
  2. Connect your league. On Sleeper, type your username and pick it from the list. ESPN and Yahoo are on the way.
  3. Tell us the lore. The running jokes, the punishment, who has never won. This is what makes it yours rather than generic.
  4. Send it round. Every edition is a link anyone can open — no app, no sign-in, nothing to install.

The first edition takes about thirty seconds to write.

Your league has to be viewable to the public

ESPN doesn’t offer a way for apps to sign in on your behalf, so the only way we can read your scores is if your league is set to be viewable. Sleeper needs none of this — it’s an ESPN thing.

This does not let anyone join your league. ESPN’s own wording: it lets non-members view league pages when given a direct link. League Manager leagues stay private to join, and nobody can see your team management, transactions or messages.
  1. Go to the League tab on ESPN Fantasy.
  2. Click Settings.
  3. Select Basic Settings.
  4. Click Edit Basic Settings.
  5. Change the public viewability option to Yes.
  6. Click Save Changes.

Only the League Manager can change this. If that isn’t you, send them these six steps — it takes about twenty seconds, and you can turn it back off whenever you like.

Rather not make it public?

Reasonable. There is another route — ESPN private leagues can be read with two values copied out of your browser’s developer tools — but those are credentials for your whole ESPN account, not just one league, and they expire every few weeks. We haven’t built it, and we’d rather not ask you for that unless enough people want it.

How it’s measured

  • Schedule luck Who you drew, and when

    Every week, how many teams in your league you’d have beaten. Second-highest score and a loss? That’s the schedule.

  • Player luck Booms and busts

    Your starters against their own averages. Your RB1 putting up two when you needed him counts against you.

  • The receipts Why it happened

    Opponents who played above themselves against you, close games, and benches that handed you a win.

  • Not luck Your lineup

    Points you left on your own bench aren’t bad luck. We show them anyway, separately.

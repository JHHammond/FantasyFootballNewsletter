{# The thirty seconds (redone 27 Oct). Generating a paper takes about half a minute. This covers the page the moment the form is submitted, shows the paper being set, and goes away when the finished paper (or an error) comes back. John, 27 Oct: the old football "does not work on mobile", and "can we make that animation better actually? It is from pretty early." Why it didn't move on a phone: the form used to be posted the ordinary way, and a phone's browser stops animating a page as soon as it starts loading the next one: the overlay froze, or never painted. Now the form is posted in the background, so this page stays alive and animating for the whole wait, and when the answer arrives the browser goes wherever the server sent it (the finished paper, or back here with a message). Every existing error path still lands where it did, because the server's answers haven't changed; only who follows the redirect has. Without fetch (very old browsers) the form posts the old way. Attach it to a form with data-generating. #}
